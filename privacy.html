Golden Hour

Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Golden Hour collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your location

If you tap Near me, the app asks iPhone for your location to sort happy hours by distance and show you on the map. Your location is used on your phone only. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it isn't stored.

What stays on your phone

Saved places, your thumbs up or down, places you add yourself and the area you last picked are kept on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

To stay current, the app downloads data from a few places. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address with each request. We don't receive or keep it.

  • The happy hour list, from GitHub (github.com), where the list is hosted.
  • Map tiles, from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org).
  • Venue photos, straight from each venue's own website.
  • Fonts and the map's code, from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com).

Tapping Directions opens Apple Maps, and tapping a venue's website opens it in Safari. Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies.

When you contact us

If you send a problem report or an email, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to fix the listing and reply to you, and we don't share it.

Children

Golden Hour is about bars and drinks and is meant for adults 21 and over. It isn't directed at children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: goldenhourla@icloud.com.
