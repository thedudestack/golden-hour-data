Golden Hour

Happy hours across LA, right now

Golden Hour shows which bars and restaurants in Los Angeles County have a happy hour on right now, starting soon or later today, with their deals and prices.

Need help, or found a mistake?

Email goldenhourla@icloud.com. In the app, open a place and tap Report a problem: it fills in which listing you mean.

Where the happy hours come from

Every listing is read from the venue's own website. Each week, every venue's page is checked again, and listings that changed are updated. Deals still change without notice, so please confirm with the bar before you order.

For bars and restaurants

If your listing is wrong, you'd rather we use a different photo, or you'd like your photo or listing removed, email goldenhourla@icloud.com from your venue's address or with a link to your site. We usually fix it within a few days.
